Insurer: Example Health Insurance Services Ltd.
Policy Name: SecureCare Health Plan
Policy Type: Individual Health Insurance
Policy Period: 01 April 2026 to 31 March 2027
Annual Premium: ₹12,000
Sum Insured: ₹5,00,000
Deductible: ₹10,000

COVERAGE

The insurer shall indemnify the insured for eligible hospitalization expenses incurred during the policy period, subject to the terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this policy.

Hospitalization expenses are covered when the insured is admitted for at least 24 consecutive hours, except for day-care procedures specifically listed by the insurer.

WAITING PERIOD

A general waiting period of 30 days applies from the policy commencement date, except for hospitalization arising from an accident.

Pre-existing diseases are subject to a waiting period of 24 months from the policy commencement date.

EXCLUSIONS

The policy does not cover cosmetic or plastic surgery unless medically necessary following an accident or as part of treatment for a covered medical condition.

Expenses related to treatment of conditions specifically excluded in the policy schedule are not payable.

DEDUCTIBLE

The insured must pay the first ₹10,000 of eligible expenses during each policy year. The insurer will consider eligible expenses above the deductible subject to policy limits and conditions.

CLAIM REQUIREMENTS

The insured should notify the insurer of planned hospitalization as early as reasonably possible. For reimbursement claims, original bills, discharge summaries, prescriptions and other documents requested by the insurer may be required.

IMPORTANT LIMIT

Room rent is covered up to ₹5,000 per day unless a different limit is specified in the policy schedule.

RENEWAL

The policy may be renewed annually subject to the insurer's renewal terms and applicable conditions.
